    ⚖️ Responsible AI & Ethical Checklist

    • 1. Data Privacy: Never pass passwords, secrets, PII, or confidential enterprise data to third-party models.
    • 2. Human-in-the-Loop: The AI response is presented as an editable suggestion rather than an enforced final decision.
    • 3. Explicit Transparency: Clear disclaimers notify the user that content was generated by an AI assistant.
    • 4. Graceful Degradation: If the external AI service fails, times out, or quota runs out, the core Task application continues operating smoothly without crashing.
